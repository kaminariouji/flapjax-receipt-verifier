FlapJax receipt verifier

Paste a buyback transaction. Every field is read from BNB Chain in your browser — no server, no key, no account.

Default: the 2026-10-16 buyback.
Any field present here is compared against the chain. Missing fields are reported as SKIP, never as PASS.
Reads eth_getTransactionReceipt and decodes the FLAPJAX Transfer logs from the pool to the treasury.
